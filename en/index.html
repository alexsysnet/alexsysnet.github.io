01PERSONAL PROFILE

About me

I am Alex Salanova, born in Barcelona (Spain). I consider myself responsible, ambitious and proactive. I grew up with a computer at home, so technology has always felt natural to me. Over time, that familiarity and curiosity about computing grew into a desire to learn, understand and solve increasingly complex problems.

In 2023, I decided to turn that curiosity into a career and began the Intermediate Vocational Training in Microcomputer Systems and Networks (SMR). I completed it in 2025 while combining my studies with a physically demanding role in cruise operations at the Port of Barcelona. That period taught me to organise my time, adapt to changing schedules and stay committed to both responsibilities, even when the pace was very demanding.

During the final stage of the programme, I had my first opportunity in IT as an IT & OT Helpdesk technician at APM Terminals Barcelona. Since February 2026, I have been studying the Higher Vocational Training in Networked Computer Systems Administration (ASIR) and the Cybersecurity Specialisation Course. Since September, I have been working as an IT Support Technician at Justnet Systems in an MSP environment, where I continue to gain experience while gradually progressing towards systems and network administration.
